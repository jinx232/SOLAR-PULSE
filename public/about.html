Engineering Philosophy

Built for honest solar sizing, not sales quotas.

Solar Pulse was founded to eliminate the guesswork, overselling, and undersized battery banks that plague residential and commercial clean energy projects across emerging markets.

The Industry Problem

Most consumers and facility managers receive quotes from solar vendors based on crude rules of thumb. Vendors routinely underestimate air conditioning and pumping motor inrush currents, omit battery Depth-of-Discharge (DoD) degradation, and quote undersized inverters that trip under everyday surges.

The consequence is expensive premature battery failure, frequent blackout interruptions, and customer distrust in clean renewable energy.

Our Engineering Solution

Solar Pulse provides transparent, physics-grounded modeling directly to property owners, solar engineers, and commercial developers before any equipment is purchased.

  • 1
    Motor Surge Awareness: Automatic calculation of locked-rotor inductive surges for pumps, refrigeration, and air conditioning.
  • 2
    True Usable Battery Autonomy: Accurate chemistry-based sizing (Lithium LiFePO4 85-90% DoD vs. Lead-Acid 50% DoD) for overnight loads.
  • 3
    Regional Irradiance Baselines: Grounded in real NASA/NREL solar radiation metrics (e.g. 5.4h average peak sun hours across West Africa).
