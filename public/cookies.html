Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Solar Pulse uses cookies and local browser storage technologies to provide, customize, and secure your experience on our platform.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. Local Storage is a modern web standard that allows applications to store key-value data directly in your browser without transmitting it on every network request.

2. Technologies We Use

Key / Cookie Type Purpose
sp_theme Local Storage Remembers your preference between Dark Mode and Light Mode.
solar_pulse_chat_* Local Storage Stores your session conversation with the Solar AI Advisor locally on your device so it is preserved across page refreshes and scoped to your user ID.
Firebase Auth Token IndexedDB / Cookie Maintains your authenticated session securely so you don't have to log in on every visit.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can manage or disable cookies and local storage through your browser settings. However, disabling essential session tokens will prevent you from signing in or saving your solar projects.

4. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory modifications. Updates are published immediately on this page.
