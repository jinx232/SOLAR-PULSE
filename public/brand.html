Trademark Specimen Description

Anatomy of the Solar Pulse™ Emblem

  • 12 Geometric Cardinal Rays: Arranged at exact 30° radial intervals, symbolizing both the 12 hours of equatorial solar irradiance and 12 months of annual energy yield modeling.
  • Dual Concentric Corona Rings: Represent continuous battery storage autonomy and protective isolation from utility grid blackouts.
  • Central Oscilloscope Pulse Waveform: A 7-vertex bi-phasic power wave (`M28,50 → L45,34 → L51,64 → L72,50`) representing live inverter heartbeat telemetry and financial ROI vitality.
Spatial Geometry

Clear Space & Minimum Scale Rules

  • Minimum Clear Zone (`0.25X`): Always maintain an unobstructed perimeter equal to at least 25% of the emblem's height (`X`) on all four sides.
  • Minimum Digital Scale: Emblem only: `24px × 24px` (favicons). Full horizontal lockup: `140px` width minimum.
  • Minimum Physical Print Scale: Emblem only: `8mm × 8mm`. Full horizontal lockup: `32mm` width minimum.

06 / Brand Governance

Logo & Identity Usage Rules

Consistent presentation strengthens our statutory and common-law trademark protection globally. Adhere strictly to these six standards.

✓ Do

Use Master Vector Files

Always scale the logo proportionally from the official `.SVG` master files provided in Section 02 above.

✓ Do

Include the ™ Symbol

Apply the `™` symbol (`Solar Pulse™`) on the first or most prominent appearance in headlines, footers, and press materials.

✓ Do

Match Lockup to Background

Use the Dark Lockup on obsidian/slate surfaces and the Light Lockup on white paper or light-mode documents.

✕ Don't

Never Alter the Pulse Waveform

Do not flatten, invert, or animate the central 7-vertex oscilloscope waveform in ways that change its recognizable silhouette.

✕ Don't

Never Substitute Brand Colors

Do not recolor the sun rays into blue, purple, or neon green. For single-color applications, use the official Monochrome White stencil.

✕ Don't

Never Imply False Endorsement

Third-party installers and hardware vendors may not combine the Solar Pulse emblem with their own company name into a single composite mark.
