Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Solar Pulse, your privacy and data security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, mobile application, or use our solar modeling and estimation tools in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable international privacy frameworks.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, password hash (managed securely via Firebase Authentication), and optional profile avatars.
  • System Sizing & Project Inputs: Data regarding appliance loads, wattage, usage hours, regional solar zones, postal codes, and roof dimensions that you configure within our Calculator and Estimator tools.
  • Transaction Data: If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is performed directly through PCI-DSS certified providers (Paystack or Flutterwave). We never store your payment card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Session telemetry, browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically for security, error diagnostics, and presence synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate accurate solar panel, inverter, and battery capacity recommendations.
  • To generate 25-year financial ROI projections and export project reports.
  • To manage your account, authenticate sessions, and track subscription entitlements.
  • To respond to support tickets, user feedback, and inquiry requests.
  • To ensure application security, mitigate fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure and processors strictly to operate our platform:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Secure authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and hosting.
  • Paystack & Flutterwave: Secure subscription billing and payment verification.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): Geographic lookup for solar irradiance coordination.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Regardless of your jurisdiction, you have full ownership over your data:

  • Access & Correction: You may review and update your profile details at any time in the app settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated project data. You can delete your account directly inside the Solar Pulse Profile settings or by emailing support@solarpulse.io. All records, saved solar projects, and auth credentials will be purged permanently.

5. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@solarpulse.io or submit a ticket through our in-app Support tool.
