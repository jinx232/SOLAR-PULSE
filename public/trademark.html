3. Prohibited Uses (Anti-Infringement)

To avoid confusing customers and preventing dilution of our brand, the following actions are strictly prohibited without prior written license from Solar Pulse Technologies Ltd:

  • Confusingly Similar Names: Using "Solar Pulse", "SolarPulse", or phonetically indistinguishable marks in connection with solar estimation, renewable energy apps, energy calculators, or hardware accessories.
  • Domain Squatting: Registering domain names, social media handles, or app store listings containing "Solar Pulse" without authorization.
  • Logo Alteration & Cloning: Copying, modifying, or imitating our SVG logo, favicon, color gradients, or UI typography.
  • False Endorsement: Using our mark in a way that falsely implies sponsorship, endorsement, or partnership by Solar Pulse.
  • Code & Algorithm Plagiarism: Scraping, reverse-engineering, or decompiling our proprietary RAG intelligence base or 25-year compounding cashflow logic.

4. Permitted Nominative Use & Official Brand Kit

Partners, journalists, researchers, and certified solar installers may refer to Solar Pulse under nominative fair use, provided that:

  • The reference is truthful, accurate, and not misleading.
  • The trademark symbol (™) is applied on the first or most prominent occurrence (e.g., "Solar Pulse™").
  • A standard attribution disclaimer is included: "Solar Pulse™ is a trademark of Solar Pulse Technologies Ltd."
  • Official master logos, color tokens, and press boilerplates are sourced unmodified from our Official Brand Identity & Press Kit (/brand.html).
