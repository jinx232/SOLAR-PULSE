# SaaS — Human Brief
## What It Is
SaaS is the dominant design language of modern B2B software — blue-dominant palettes, white card-based layouts, generous whitespace, and a relentless focus on clarity and trust. It's the visual equivalent of a firm handshake: professional, dependable, and conversion-optimized.
## Why It Exists
The SaaS design pattern crystallized in the 2010s as companies like Stripe, Linear, and Vercel raised the bar for developer tools and B2B products. Blue became the trust color; cards became the organizing principle; and the hero-with-screenshot layout became the universal landing page template.
## Best For
B2B SaaS products, developer tools, fintech dashboards, project management apps, API platforms, and any product selling trust alongside functionality.
