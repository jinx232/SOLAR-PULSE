## 3. Jurisdiction-Specific Filing Procedures

### A. Nigeria (Primary Operating Market)
- **Registry:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry, Commercial Law Department, Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment (FMITI), Abuja.
- **Filing Method:** Can be filed through an accredited Trademark Agent / Lawyer or via the [FMITI Online IP Portal](https://iponigeria.com/).
- **Step-by-Step Procedure:**
  1. **Availability Search (Form 1):** Conduct an official search on the IP Nigeria registry database to ensure no prior identical or confusingly similar mark exists in Classes 9 and 42. (Takes 1–3 business days; cost ~₦5,000–₦10,000).
  2. **Application for Registration (Form 2):** Submit word mark "Solar Pulse" and logo image, applicant details (Solar Pulse Technologies Ltd or Founder name), and specification of goods/services.
  3. **Acknowledgement & Acceptance:** The Registry issues an official **Acknowledgement Slip** (instant proof of filing date) followed by an **Acceptance Letter** (within 2–4 weeks). *At this point, you hold priority rights against subsequent filers.*
  4. **Publication in the Trademark Journal:** The mark is published in the official Trademark Journal for a 2-month statutory opposition period.
  5. **Issuance of Certificate of Registration:** If no opposition is filed, the Registrar issues your final **Certificate of Trademark Registration**, valid for 7 years and renewable indefinitely every 14 years.
- **Estimated Official Government Filing Fees:** Approximately ₦15,000 – ₦25,000 per class (official fee). Legal agent representation typically costs ₦60,000 – ₦150,000.

### B. United States (USPTO)
- **Registry:** United States Patent and Trademark Office ([USPTO.gov](https://www.uspto.gov)).
- **Filing Basis:**
  - **Section 1(a) (Use in Commerce):** Since the web platform is live and accessible globally at `https://solar-pulse1.web.app` with paying users, file under 1(a) with screenshots of the live app and pricing page as your specimen of use.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Use the **TEAS Plus** filing option ($250 per class) to minimize costs.
  2. The USPTO Examining Attorney conducts a review within 4–6 months.
  3. The mark is published in the Trademark Official Gazette for a 30-day opposition window.
  4. Notice of Registration issued.
- **Official Fee:** $250 – $350 per class.

### C. International Protection via the Madrid Protocol (WIPO)
- Once you file your basic application in Nigeria or the US, you can file an **International Trademark Application** through the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) under the Madrid System, designating up to 130 member countries with a single application.
