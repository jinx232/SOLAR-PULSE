# Clean — Human Brief
## What It Is
A rigorously minimal design approach where content is the only ornament. White backgrounds, grayscale-plus-one-accent palettes, strict 12-column grids, and generous negative space combine to create interfaces that feel invisible — users focus entirely on the content, never on the container.
## Why It Exists
The apotheosis of the "less is more" tradition: from Dieter Rams' 10 principles of good design through Apple's rise, Google's Material Design, and the flat design revolution. Clean design is the default answer when you don't know what else to do — and the hardest to do well, because there's nothing to hide behind.
## Best For
SaaS products, documentation, news sites, government services, healthcare apps, enterprise tools, and any product where clarity and usability are non-negotiable.
